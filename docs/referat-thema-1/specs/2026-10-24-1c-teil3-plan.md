**Goal:** Close the remaining model-free 1C items:

- end-to-end cases E10–E15: busy, queue full, CONFLICT, legacy-text success and drop, full #456 context;
- measurements M6 (costs with full context) and M7 (share of `source_changed` under voting);

**Architecture:** Extend the existing suites. Cases go into `qa.summary-e2e.pg.test.ts`, the full-context helper into `__tests__/lib/qa-summary-pg-fixture.ts`, the legacy mode into `__tests__/lib/open-weight-test-runtime.ts`, and the measurements into `qa.summary-measurement.pg.test.ts` with an extended `auswertung.mjs`. Evidence goes into a new protocol `docs/referat-thema-1/evidenz/1C/2026-10-24-teil3.md`.

**Spec:** [2026-10-24-1c-teil3-design.md](2026-10-24-1c-teil3-design.md), pre-registered in `fb94160d`.

## Global Constraints

Same as part 2 ([plan](2026-10-24-1c-teil2-plan.md#global-constraints)):

- **No production code changes.** Mutation probes are local only, reverted, and recorded with diff, red run, green run and SHA.
- **Tests:** titles without referat IDs; German `it`; English `describe` ending in `(PostgreSQL + Redis)`; no `any`.
- **Commits:** Conventional Commits, subject at most 100 characters, no AI attribution.
- **Runtime:** Node 24.18.0 via `mise exec node@24.18.0 --`. Local services are the Podman containers `arsnova-1c-pg` and `arsnova-1c-redis`.
- **Pre-registered H/G/K:** no change without a recorded reason. If K fails on unchanged code, stop and report.
- **Integrity of earlier evidence:** the recorded JSON of parts 1 and 2 stays unchanged. New measurements go into new files.

## Review Focus

1. **E12:** the vote must happen during the real build. The wrapper sits only at the Redis system boundary and passes everything through. The control without the vote must stay `pending`.
2. **E15:** every area must be `available` with content. Check the raw body, not only the preview.
3. **E11:** jobs of three separate sessions. Count the POSTs; nothing may be left after the latch is released.
4. **M7:** a seeded Poisson process. Measure T per run, derive the prediction per run, and report Wilson intervals.
5. **Load:** no other test process may run during the measurements.
